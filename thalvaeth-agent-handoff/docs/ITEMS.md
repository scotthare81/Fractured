## Invisible quality tiers

Every craftable carries a **hidden quality** (the Model C roll — low→high) that is **never shown**: no rarity colour, no stars, no number. You read it off the **name/description and the effect**.

| Hidden quality | The player sees | Effect |
|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| low | *"Thin, watery stew"* | Barely dents Hunger; spoils fast |
| mid | *"Stew"* | Solid meal |
| high | *"Hearty, rich stew"* | Fills more, lasts longer |

The same holds for everything — food restore, tincture potency, blade sharpness & durability, armour mitigation. Quality = **input quality + the roll** (no skill levels — L8): a fresh Tusker haunch reads toward *"hearty,"* stringy hound meat toward *"thin."* You judge by feel and flavour text, never a badge (L9/L10).

## 3. Forage & mushrooms

Free everywhere; yield by district. Discovery risk: a wrong pick **sickens or poisons** you — learn the tells.

**Foraged foods**

| Item | Note |
|------|------|
| Tallowroot / Tubers | Starch staple |
| Wild Grain | → flour → bread/porridge |
| Nuts / Acorns | Leach bitterness → flour |
| Wild Greens · Roots | Bulk, light Hunger |
| Berries · Rosehip | Snack, a little Thirst; spoils |
| Wild Honey | Rich Hunger + sweetener — **bees bite** |
| Bird Eggs | From nests; good food |
| Grubs / Insects | Survival protein (grim) |
| Cattail / Reed | Marsh starch |
| Lichen | Famine food |

**Mushrooms** — three classes, and you don't know which by looking until you learn it:

| Fungus | Class | Use |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Brown Cap · Button | Edible | Food |
| Morel · Goldgill | Edible (choice) | Better food |
| Bloodcap | Medicinal | Styptic (stop bleeding) |
| Fevergill | Medicinal | Fever / Infection |
| **Gravecap** | **Poison** | Poison ingredient — deadly to eat |
| **Palecap · Weeping Fungus** | **Poison** | Poison ingredient |

Poison caps feed §7. Eating one is the flip side of the risk.

## 4. Fishing

A quiet food source (low noise — good for stealth). Ponds/streams in runs are the spots.

**Tackle:** Fishing Line (crude) → Rod · Hook (bone → metal) · Net · **Fish Trap / Weir** (passive catch).

**Catch** (raw fish — perishable, satchel + spoil timer; quality tiers apply — a fat eel beats a bony minnow): River Fish · Perch · Catfish · **Eel** · Bottomfeeder · Minnows.

**Products:** cook → Grilled Fish / Fish Stew · smoke → **Smoked Fish** (long shelf) · render → Fish Oil (lamp/food) · **Roe** (bait/food) · Fish Skin (light leather) · Fish Bone (needle/glue).

## 7. Poisons (mundane toxins — no magic)

Made at the ash-still from poison fungi/plants — a Rogue-chassis staple, **discovery-gated** (advanced apothecary). Mostly **applied, not drunk**.

| Poison | From | Does |
|--------|------|------|
| Weakening Coat | Gravecap + oil | Coat a blade → weaken / DoT on hit |
| Necrotic Coat | Palecap + alum | Coat → bleed / rot damage |
| Sedative Draught | Duskflower | Sleep / calm — subdue, or dose bait |
| Poisoned Bait | Meat + poison cap | Kill / weaken beasts or roster that eats it |
| Choking Smoke | Sulfur + fungus | Area deterrent / flush |

Flip side: eating poison forage, or a bad dose, hurts **you** — the discovery risk cuts both ways.

## 9. Traps

You place these in a run. **Food traps** gather passively; **combat traps** control the roster. Some need metal (forge-gated).

| Trap | Type | Does | Needs |
|------|------|------|-------|
| Snare | food | Catch small game (hare) | Cord |
| Fish Trap / Weir | food | Passive fish | Cord / reeds |
| Deadfall | food/combat | Crush game / stagger enemy | Wood + bait |
| Pit / Stake Pit | combat | Fall damage / impale | Dig + stakes |
| Net Trap | combat | Immobilise | Rope |
| Jaw Trap | combat | Snap-hold + damage | **Metal (forge)** |
| Tripwire + Alarm | utility | Warn you | Cord + bone/bell |
| Tripwire + Spikes | combat | Damage on cross | Cord + stakes |
