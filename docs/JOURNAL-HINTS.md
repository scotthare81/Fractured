# Journal hint library

Canonical copy for **near-miss discovery hints** in the Journal Record.

A failed experiment may add one of these notes to the Remnant's Journal **only when the attempt is meaningfully close to a real recipe or process**. Random ingredient piles do not produce useful hints. Repeating the same mistake does not create duplicate knowledge.

Hints describe **properties, process, or failure**. They do **not** expose exact missing ingredients, exact quantities, recipe names, or hidden implementation rules.

Good: *"Too wet to hold together."*  
Bad: *"Missing 1 Grave Salt."*

Good: *"The heat never reached the centre."*  
Bad: *"Requires Forge tier 2."*

## 19. Hint persistence rules

1. A hint is stored by a stable internal **hint key**, not by raw text.
2. A Remnant learns a given hint key once.
3. Repeating the same near-miss may show a short transient failure line, but **must not add another Journal entry**.
4. One failed craft should usually reveal **one** new hint. Two is exceptional.
5. The system should prefer the **least revealing useful hint** first.
6. Stronger hints unlock only after the player has already learned broader ones, or after a closer attempt.
7. Exact ingredient names are only shown when that ingredient/property is already known to the Remnant.
8. Random invalid recipes produce no persistent hint.
9. Hints may attach to a recipe silhouette, a known material, a gear node, or a general technique entry.
10. Success resolves the silhouette to the learned recipe/process and retains earlier hints as field notes/history where useful.

## 20. Hint-strength ladder

Recommended three-step ladder for a discoverable recipe:

| Strength | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Broad | *"Wouldn't bind."* | One process/property is wrong |
| Directional | *"It needs something to hold it together."* | Points at the class of solution |
| Near-success | *"The structure works; one part is still wrong."* | Player is very close |

Do not jump directly from no knowledge to an exact solution.

---

## 21. UI presentation

Persistent hints should read like field notes, not validation errors:

```text
[grey silhouette]
???

Notes:
• The liquid needs to be cleaner.
• The herb gives more after crushing.
• Almost. The process is sound.
```

Once discovered:

```text
ASH TEA

Learned process:
[known recipe presentation]

Earlier notes:
• The liquid needs to be cleaner.
• The herb gives more after crushing.
```

The player should be able to follow their own chain of reasoning after returning from a run.
