## Identity

- **Palette:** desaturated ash-grey, bone-white, rot-brown, fog. **Blood-red is the only accent** (the Sleeper's eyes).
- **Type:** weathered, archaic **serif** carved from cracked stone — grim, no glow/bevel. The apostrophe in *Thal'vaeth* is the signature.
- **Motifs:** the monastery arch, bare dead Rotwood trees, fog, stitching, and the watching red eyes.

## Production caveats

- **Rebuild the wordmark in real type.** The generator's lettering (straight *and* curved) is unreliable — verify every "Thal'vaeth" and re-set it in a real weathered-serif font, especially the seal's curved ring text.
- **Export to BLP** and to the client's fixed login-logo/splash dimensions.
- **Transparent backgrounds** for the logo/seal where they composite over the splash.
- Open look decisions (accent red vs ash-orange; crest-only vs crest + corner wordmark) are tracked in [CLIENT-BRANDING.md](../CLIENT-BRANDING.md).
